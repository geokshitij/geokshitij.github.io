I am part of the **[Living Mountains Group](https://livingmountains.github.io)**, an independent research collective of scientists and engineers working across universities, research institutes, industry, and government on four continents.

We observe, model and forecast a changing mountain world: floods and flash floods, landslides and debris flows, glacial lakes and their outburst floods, groundwater, and the resilience of mountain communities and infrastructure. We combine process-based understanding with **artificial intelligence and remote sensing**, and we mentor the next generation of mountain researchers.
